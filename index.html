01 / The principle

How an LVDT works

The core moves without touching the coils.

STEP 01

Excite the primary

An AC supply energizes the primary winding and creates a changing magnetic field.

STEP 02

Induce two voltages

The magnetic field couples into secondary windings S1 and S2.

STEP 03

Compare the outputs

The differential output indicates displacement and direction.

Vout = VS1 − VS2

This is a simplified educational simulation, not a calibrated sensor model.

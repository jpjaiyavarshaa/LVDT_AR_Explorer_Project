01 / The principle

How an LVDT works

No contact needed between the moving core and the coils.

STEP 01

Excite the primary

An AC supply energizes the primary winding, creating a changing magnetic field inside the transformer.

STEP 02

Induce two voltages

The field couples into secondary windings S1 and S2. Their induced voltages depend on the core position.

STEP 03

Compare the outputs

The secondaries are connected in series opposition. Their difference gives a signal related to displacement and direction.

Vout = VS1 − VS2

At the electrical null position, VS1 ≈ VS2, so the differential output is approximately zero. Moving the core to either side makes the magnitude increase; the output phase relative to the primary indicates direction. This is an idealized learning model, not a calibrated sensor.

04 / Real-world connection

Where are LVDTs used?

LVDTs measure linear position in test machines, hydraulic actuators, industrial automation, aircraft control systems, and precision manufacturing. The sensor converts mechanical movement into an electrical signal that a controller can interpret.

Remember: the LVDT measures displacement; signal conditioning electronics typically demodulate and scale the AC differential output for a usable position reading.
